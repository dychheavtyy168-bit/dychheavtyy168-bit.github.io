About Me

I am a Cambodian Mechanical Of Automotive Engineering graduate from the National Polytechnic Institute of Cambodia (NPIC), currently pursuing further academic development at Moscow Polytechnic University, Russia. My academic interests focus on materials science, composite materials, mechanical design, and experimental testing. I have gained hands-on experience in carbon fiber and fiberglass composite fabrication, particularly for motorcycle helmet applications. My research on composite materials has been published in STI Focus, highlighting opportunities and challenges for composite material development in Cambodia..
